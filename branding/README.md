# ResCenter — Brand Identity

**Name:** ResCenter
**Tagline:** *Radiology Research Data Platform*
**File location:** `branding/`

## Visual Direction

The brand is **holographic, dark, medical-AI** — inspired by futuristic radiology viewers (think holographic CT workstations, *Ghost in the Shell* med-bays). Built on a **dark teal/navy** base with **glowing cyan/teal** accents.

## Mark Anatomy

The icon tile contains four layers, mirroring the reference image:

1. **Holographic floor grid** — repeating teal lines at 22% opacity, with a radial vignette darkening the corners. Reads as a 3D scanned volume.
2. **Glowing scan reticle** — two concentric rings + 4 crosshair ticks + center dot, all in `#5EEAD4` with a soft Gaussian glow.
3. **Tilted glass slice panels** — two translucent rounded rectangles with mini skeleton silhouettes and a "SLICE 112/114" label. **Direct visual reference to your screenshot.**
4. **Database cylinder** — chrome-teal 3-disc cylinder with a glowing cyan atomic orbit + electron + nucleus, representing storage + research + AI.
5. **Corner AI dot** — small `#5EEAD4` orb with a dark core, floating in the top-right.

## Typography

- **Wordmark:** Inter 700, -2 letter-spacing, color `#ECFEFF` (ice white).
- **Tagline:** Inter 500, +4 letter-spacing, color `#5EEAD4` (mint glow).

## Usage Notes

- The mark is designed to **live on dark backgrounds** (`#0A1F2A` or darker). For light backgrounds, use the `mono` variant inverted to dark text.
- Minimum icon size: **48×48**. Below that, drop the slice panels and keep only the reticle + cylinder.
- The cylinder, orbit, and slice panels should never be separated — they're a single lockup.
